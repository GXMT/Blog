This page is a **proof of concept** for the interactive 3D viewer.

Click **"Load interactive 3D viewer"** above to render the build in your browser —
you can drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and inspect it from any angle. The heavy
3D engine only downloads when you click, so the rest of the site stays fast.

Once this works, every farm guide can embed its own schematic the same way:
readers get a hands-on 3D model instead of a video they can't pause or spin.
